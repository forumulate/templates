# Beacon Waitlist

**An official Forumulate template.** Modelled on: a pre-launch product’s waitlist page.

A promise, three reasons and a short waitlist form.

## What applying it creates

- **The look** — the `product` custom look.
- **The form** — email, role and a scale, as a page form.

Submissions land in the space's forms inbox. The brand is `Your Brand`, replaced with your organization's name.
