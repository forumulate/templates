# Shortlist Roundups

An editorial site of category roundups and matrices for a publication with no product of its own, with the facts and their sources shown on every page.

A COMPARE template written directly as data (no replica): a sample catalog of fictional products, comparison pages drawn from it and a `Your Brand` placeholder. Every fact carries a sample verified date; re-check and re-date them before publishing (Freshness panel in the studio).

- A ranked roundup and a full matrix for the category
- No product of your own: a roundup site is a valid catalog
- Editorial front page: the roundups lead, in cards
- Ratings only ever from third parties, never awarded by you
- Zero custom CSS — every detail is a theme setting
