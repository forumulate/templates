# Lumen Launch

**An official Forumulate custom-page template.** Modelled on: a. product-planning tool’s dark homepage

A dark product homepage: gradient display type, a split hero with a product shot in perspective, a logo wall, alternating feature rows, a five-cell bento, a changelog teaser and a closing call to action under a blurred sticky header with a mega menu.

## What it uses

- A landing page: the space’s address shows it, full width
- Split hero with a perspective product shot
- Logo wall, alternating feature rows, five-cell bento
- Changelog teaser (pick your changelog in the feed’s settings)
- Gradient display type and rise-in motion
- Blurred sticky header with a mega menu, 4-column footer
- Zero custom CSS — every detail is a theme setting
- Type: Inter (mirrored to our own font store, never loaded from a third party)
- Colour scheme: dark

## What applying it creates

- **The look** — `bundle.json`, a theme bundle: palette, type, motion, header and footer. Published to the space straight away.
- **The layout** — the `landing` preset.
- **1 page** — built from section bands, heroes and the section library’s blocks, published:
  - Home — the landing page itself
- **Pictures** — 12 file(s) in `assets/`, copied into your library.

“Your Brand” becomes your organization’s name wherever it appears, the wordmark included.
