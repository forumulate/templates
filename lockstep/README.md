# Lockstep

**An official Forumulate FAQ template.** Modelled on: a company’s internal IT and workplace help desk.

An internal IT help desk: categories with sections nested inside them, each section its own page of answers, related questions under every answer, and a request form for whatever is not covered.

Screenshots are taken from the live preview after deploy (`screenshots/desktop.png`, `screenshots/phone.png`).

## What it uses

- Preset: help centre — search hero, category tiles, popular questions
- Nested sections: Accounts › Passwords, Devices › Laptops and phones
- Each section is its own page: an accordion of its questions, the trail back, its sub-sections listed
- Under every answer: related questions, the rest of its section, and “email me when this changes”
- Pinned and badged questions; an Updated chip on anything changed this fortnight
- Slate and teal, Inter, square corners
- Zero custom CSS — every detail is a theme setting
