# Northstar Story

**An official Forumulate custom-page template.** Modelled on: a. browser company’s editorial product story

A long-form product story in large serif type: a full-bleed film behind the title, a numbers band, a scroll-driven sequence where the picture stays put while the chapters pass, pull quotes and parallax picture breaks.

## What it uses

- A landing page: the space’s address shows it, full width
- Full-bleed film behind the title (muted, looping; the poster under reduced motion)
- Scroll-driven story: the picture stays while the chapters pass
- Numbers band, pull quotes, parallax picture breaks
- Large serif display type over a clean sans
- Zero custom CSS — every detail is a theme setting
- Type: Newsreader and Inter (mirrored to our own font store, never loaded from a third party)
- Colour scheme: light

## What applying it creates

- **The look** — `bundle.json`, a theme bundle: palette, type, motion, header and footer. Published to the space straight away.
- **The layout** — the `landing` preset.
- **1 page** — built from section bands, heroes and the section library’s blocks, published:
  - The story — film, chapters, quotes and picture breaks
- **Pictures** — 11 file(s) in `assets/`, copied into your library.

“Your Brand” becomes your organization’s name wherever it appears, the wordmark included.
