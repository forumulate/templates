# Tidepool Pricing

**An official Forumulate custom-page template.** Modelled on: a. SaaS company’s pricing page

A light pricing page: a centred headline, three plans with a monthly / yearly switch, a comparison table whose header stays put as you scroll, an FAQ and an enterprise band with a contact form that lands in your inbox.

## What it uses

- A landing page: the space’s address shows it, full width
- Three plans with a monthly / yearly switch that needs no JavaScript
- Comparison table with a sticky header
- FAQ that opens one answer at a time
- Enterprise band with a real contact form, created on apply
- Zero custom CSS — every detail is a theme setting
- Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (mirrored to our own font store, never loaded from a third party)
- Colour scheme: system (light, with a dark twin)

## What applying it creates

- **The look** — `bundle.json`, a theme bundle: palette, type, motion, header and footer. Published to the space straight away.
- **The layout** — the `landing` preset.
- **1 page** — built from section bands, heroes and the section library’s blocks, published:
  - Pricing — plans, comparison, FAQ and the enterprise form
- **Pictures** — 8 file(s) in `assets/`, copied into your library.
- **A form** — created in your organization and wired to the page’s form block.

“Your Brand” becomes your organization’s name wherever it appears, the wordmark included.
