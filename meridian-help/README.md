# Meridian Help

**An official Forumulate template.** Modelled on: a global company’s customer-support directory.

Topic cards first, then that topic's form; offices with opening hours; the other ways to get help.

## What applying it creates

- **The look** — the `directory` contact look, centred head, outlined office cards.
- **The layout** — the `router` preset: a card per topic, each with its own address (`/<mount>/billing`).
- **Topics** — Billing, Technical support (asks product and severity), Account access.
- **The page the copy lives on** — intro, email, response time, three offices and opening hours.

Map links open a search, never a tile: nothing on the page calls a map provider. The brand is `Your Brand`, replaced with your organization's name.
